External Reporting & statutory returns

Statutory reporting centre

A country-driven report centre that auto-builds filing-ready statutory returns from your ERP data, localized to your registration country (UAE by default: FTA, MOHRE, goAML, MoEC). One catalogue covers tax, financial, audit, HR/WPS, AML and corporate filings — each in the official format with full sample data so nothing is ever blank.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Corporate Tax return (full computation)

The complete UAE Corporate Tax return under Federal Decree-Law 47/2022 — taxpayer & period, elections/reliefs (Small Business Relief, QFZP 0%/9%, group transfers), accounting profit reconciled to taxable income through add-backs, interest limitation and loss relief, then 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above, with six supporting schedules and tax-group/intercompany handling.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Drill-down to transaction level

Every figure on a return is clickable in place: a box or computation line expands to its breakdown, a line expands to its invoices or entries, and a single invoice expands to its individual supply / GL lines — recursively, until you reach the individual transaction behind the number.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Built-in compliance engine

Each return is validated against the law with PASS / REVIEW / FAIL flags — VAT special schemes (24kt investment gold 0%, B2B gold/diamond reverse charge, residential lease exempt) and CT conditions (non-deductibles, 30% EBITDA interest cap, exempt income, 75% loss relief, transfer pricing) — so nothing is left to check before filing.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Flexible reporting periods

Each report carries its own statutory period model — VAT monthly or quarterly, Corporate Tax by financial year (including non-calendar / transitional first periods via a custom range), WPS monthly, financials annual. A Run / Recalculate control rebuilds the return for the exact period selected.

External Reporting & statutory returns

FTA-style audit-file schedules

One-click Excel/CSV supporting schedules in the FTA layout — VAT invoice-wise, customer TRN-wise, supplier-wise and adjustments; CT adjustments, fixed-asset depreciation, exempt income, related-party / transfer pricing, tax losses and foreign tax credit.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Professional PDF + commentary

A colour, MIS-style filing pack with cover page, section numbering, the summary schedules and a plain-language "report explained" commentary on both VAT and CT so a learner understands every figure. Invoice-level detail is excluded so a 10k-invoice tenant still gets a clean summary.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Off-system Excel import → return

Check or file other clients without touching the ERP: download a multi-sheet Excel template (company & TRN, boxes/lines, invoice detail, compliance, instructions), fill it, upload, and the tool builds the full VAT, Corporate Tax or IFRS financial-statements report and PDF entirely from the uploaded data.

External Reporting & statutory returns

External Audit Report (ISA 700 / IFRS)

A complete IFRS assurance pack with a professional cover page and table of contents: the Independent Auditor's Report (opinion, basis for opinion, going concern, key audit matters, responsibilities — ISA 700/701/705/570/720) and all four primary statements with prior-year comparatives — financial position, profit or loss & OCI, changes in equity and cash flows — plus detailed notes referencing each IAS/IFRS standard, drill-down to transaction level and a Fetch button to refresh the standards.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Financial model & 5-year forecast

A five-year financial model built from the live GL actuals — an explicit assumptions block (growth, margins, capex, working capital, tax, WACC), a projected profit & loss, an EBITDA and free-cash-flow build and key ratios. The free-cash-flow line drives the business valuation so the two stay consistent.

External Reporting & statutory returns

Business valuation report

Values the business by three methods on the same model — discounted cash flow (WACC + Gordon terminal value), market multiples (EV/EBITDA and P/E) and net assets / book value — with an enterprise-value → equity-value bridge, an indicative range and a central estimate, field guide and commentary.
