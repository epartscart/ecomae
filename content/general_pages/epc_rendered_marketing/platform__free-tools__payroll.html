Guide: Payroll & Gratuity

What it does

Calculate monthly net pay and end-of-service gratuity for an employee from basic salary, allowances, deductions and years of service.

Who it's for

Employers and HR/payroll staff who need a quick net-pay and gratuity figure.

What you get

  • Gross pay, deductions and net pay
  • Statutory end-of-service gratuity for the years served
  • Country currency applied
  • Save / print the calculation

How it works

Gratuity rules and currency follow your registered country's labour law (e.g. UAE 21/30 days per year banding).
